# Poxture App

A Mac menu-bar app that checks your posture through your Mac's camera and nudges you when a bad habit holds. Download it from [poxture.com](https://poxture.com/).

## How it works

1. It checks how you sit through your Mac's camera or an external webcam, once a minute.
2. When a habit holds, a soft glow shows at the top of your screen and a card shows the fix. Looking down or slouching has to hold for about half a minute, by default.
3. The glow turns green once you've fixed it.

## The 8 habits it checks

1. Too close to the screen
2. Looking down
3. Hand on face
4. Slouching
5. Sitting still too long
6. Hunched over
7. Leaning to one side
8. Not working from a proper desk setup (on the sofa or in bed)

All 8 are on by default. You can switch any of them off.

## Privacy

No pictures, ever: Poxture App never saves, shows or sends a camera picture, and camera images never leave your Mac.

What the app sends, in short (the full list is on the privacy page at poxture.com):

1. Usage events with a random install number.
2. A daily record of posture numbers with no install number.
3. Its log, only when you send a report or say a nudge was wrong or missing.
4. Your activation key to Dodo Payments to check it, once you buy.

The only images that ever leave your Mac are screenshots you add to a report yourself.
